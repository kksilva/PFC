\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This work aims to experimentally characterize the propulsion system of the Kyosho Mad Force
Kruiser 2.0 VE radio-controlled model car, equipped with a brushless motor and an Electronic
Speed Controller (ESC). Developed at the Intelligent Systems Laboratory of the Department of
Electrical Engineering at the Federal University of Minas Gerais, within the activities of the
Autonomous Vehicles course, it seeks to develop and validate a low-cost method for the indirect
estimation of the torque produced by the motor--ESC--transmission assembly, providing support
for the development of closed-loop torque control strategies.

The methodology employs an ACS758 current sensor, connected to an Arduino Due microcontroller
for the acquisition of the electrical signals, and a digital scale to measure the traction force
during tests with the model car immobilized and its wheels in contact with the ground. Based on
the measured force and on the effective radius of the driving wheel, the torque developed by the
propulsion system is calculated, allowing its relationship with the consumed electric current to
be established. The obtained data are analyzed for agreement with the theoretical powertrain
model and subjected to a measurement uncertainty treatment, in order to determine the validity
range and reliability of the proposed estimation method. The results confirm the relationship
between torque and current within the adhesion region, with a slope of $0.372$ N/(A/u) and a
determination coefficient of $0.79$, identify a first-order current dynamics with a time
constant of $0.18$ s, and support the design and simulation-based evaluation of a cascade
torque control loop with adhesion limiting. The limitations of the method are concentrated in
the region near the ESC neutral point and in the battery supply conditions.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords}: Closed-loop control. Current sensor. Electronic
instrumentation. Measurement uncertainty. Radio-controlled model car. Torque estimation.
